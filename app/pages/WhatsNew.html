WHAT'S NEW

0.1.80

  • The plug-in looks like Beat Key. Same faceplate as the app window: key dial, BPM, top keys, keyboard, tuning, the send caps. ADVANCED adds the key wheel and chords.
  • DOWNLOAD in the plug-in. Paste a YouTube link, press DOWNLOAD. In Pro Tools, pick where it goes: New Track at the cursor, session start, song start, spot, or the Clip List. Your last picks are listed. Nothing is picked for you.
  • Named with BPM and key. The beat lands named with its tempo and key. Outside Pro Tools it is saved and shown in Finder.
  • Pro Tools import. Beat Key talks to Pro Tools over PTSL. If Pro Tools is closed or PTSL is off, the plug-in says so and keeps the file.
  • One log. Plug-in problems go to ~/Library/Logs/Beat Key/beatkey.log with the host's name. REPORT sends it.
  • Update Plug-ins. The Beat Key menu shows Update Plug-ins… when the plug-ins are older than the app.

0.1.79

  • Plug-in. Put Beat Key on a track. Play. Key and BPM show. Audio Unit, VST3 and AAX.
  • SEND BPM and SEND AUTO-TUNE. Two buttons on the plug-in. SEND BPM sets the session tempo through the Beat Key app. SEND AUTO-TUNE sets Auto-Tune's key.
  • One engine. The app and the plug-in read key and BPM with the same engine, built into Beat Key. Same answers as before. No Python inside: a smaller, faster app.
  • One download. The plug-ins are choices in the Beat Key installer. Click Customize to pick formats.
  • Update Plug-ins. After Beat Key updates, it asks once to update the plug-ins. One password.
  • Install log. The installer writes ~/Library/Logs/Beat Key/install.log.
  • Quick Keys. Press ⌃⇧K to see every shortcut. Click one and type new keys.
  • Send Report. Help › Send Report opens Mail with the log attached. No Mail: it saves to your Desktop.
  • Uninstall. Beat Key menu › Uninstall Beat Key. Everything goes to the Trash, the plug-ins too. Keep your settings if you want.
  • Manual, What's New, Licenses. Manual and What's New are in the Help menu. Licenses is in the Beat Key menu. All open without internet.
  • Quieter glow. The display lights up only when something is going on.
  • Whites follow your colour. The white keys and caps lean toward the colour you picked.
  • Darker faceplate, thinner frame. More display, less border.
  • Key wheel cards follow the mouse. No more jumping above and below the wheel.
  • Shorter Settings. Fewer words. Shortcuts live in Quick Keys.
  • Listen to is never blank. If your saved input is unplugged, it says so and listens to Mac audio.
  • Fixes. A round of small fixes from review: one key table, cleaner readout, steadier cards.
